OUR PROJECTS

CYGNUS AGRO

AGRICULTURE + TECHNOLOGY

Cygnus Agro

Cygnus Agro focuses on connecting agricultural production with technology, local enterprise and market opportunities. It responds to the need for stronger food systems and more productive agricultural activity within local communities.

Through crop production, agrotechnology and collaboration with farmers and local institutions, the project explores practical ways to improve productivity and create economic opportunities for young people.

Our goal is to develop agricultural models that support food security, strengthen local economies and create sustainable value across the agricultural supply chain.

ALPHA PROJECT

EDUCATION + AGRITECH

Alpha Project

Alpha Project connects classroom learning with practical agriculture, entrepreneurship and technology. It addresses the gap between what students learn and the productive skills they can apply in everyday life.

At Godbey Secondary School, the project includes the development of a school fish pond, creating a practical learning opportunity around fish farming, food production and agricultural enterprise.

Through school-based agricultural activities and agrotech clubs, Alpha Project aims to help students develop useful skills, explore future career opportunities and understand how knowledge can be turned into productive value.

GONG UNIVERSE

CULTURE + CREATIVE ECONOMY

Gong Universe

Gong Universe is a cultural and creative ecosystem inspired by African mythology, indigenous knowledge, history and storytelling. It responds to the need to preserve African narratives and make them accessible to new generations.

The project explores books, games, animation and digital experiences that bring African stories, deities and traditional worldviews into contemporary creative formats.

By connecting cultural preservation with technology and creative enterprise, Gong Universe aims to create learning experiences, reach audiences locally and globally, and open new economic opportunities for African creators.
